The problem

You're running a serious business
on BROKEN tools built for something else.

The average personal trainer in India juggles 6 different apps just to coach one client. Here's what that's actually costing you.

WhatsApp chaos

Workout plans buried in chat. Your personal number exposed. Zero structure. Client messages lost in personal conversations.

Google Sheets nightmares

Plans break on mobile. Tracking is manual. No logging, no adherence data, no history. Looks unprofessional to clients.

Progress photos in chats

Client transformation photos floating in WhatsApp backups forever. No privacy guarantee. DPDPA compliance? Impossible.

Manual payment collection

Chasing clients for payment every month. No invoices. No subscription tracking. No payment history. All on Paytm or UPI.

Zoom for every check-in

Scheduling video calls across WhatsApp and Zoom. No session history. No automatic reminders. Every call is a manual back-and-forth.

Calendly for scheduling

Managing sessions across Calendly, Google Calendar, and WhatsApp. Missed sessions, no count tracking, no automated reminders.

The solution

One app. Everything your
coaching business needs.

Built specifically for the personal trainer operating in India. Every feature designed to replace a broken workflow.

Workout Plans

Build plans once, assign to any client. Clients log sets, reps, and weight in real-time. Offline support with auto-sync.

Replaces Google Sheets

Diet Plans

Create meal plans with calories, macros, and food items. Smart unit defaults for Indian foods. Goal-based scaling built in.

Replaces WhatsApp PDFs

Progress Tracking

Weight, body fat, measurements, and progress photos — all in private encrypted storage. Adherence charts and streak tracking.

Replaces manual logging

Form Check Videos

Clients upload exercise videos. You review and leave timestamped feedback. No more scheduling Zoom calls for 2-minute corrections.

Replaces Zoom

Private Messaging

Real-time chat between trainer and client. No phone numbers exchanged. Purely professional. Full message history.

Replaces WhatsApp

Session Scheduling

Book, track, and manage all sessions in a calendar. Missed session detection. Automatic session count tracking per month.

Replaces manual tracking

Payment Collection

Collect client payments via Razorpay. Set autopay per client or track manually. No more chasing payments on UPI. Full payment history in one place.
