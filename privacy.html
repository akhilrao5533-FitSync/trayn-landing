01

Who I Am

Trayn is a fitness platform that connects personal trainers with their clients in India. It is operated by me, Akhil Rao Takkallapally ("I", "me", or "my"). The platform is accessible at app.gettrayn.com.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of Trayn — both personal trainers and their clients. By creating an account and using Trayn, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

02

What I Collect

I collect only what is necessary to provide the service.

Data Who Why
Full name & email Trainers & Clients Account creation and identification
Age, gender, weight, height Clients Personalised training and diet plans
Fitness goal Clients Goal-appropriate progress scoring
Workout logs Clients Tracking exercise completion and adherence
Diet logs Clients Tracking nutrition adherence
Progress photos Clients Visual progress tracking (stored privately)
Form check videos Clients Exercise form review by trainer (max 50 MB)
Session history Clients Tracking scheduled and completed sessions
Health notes Clients Trainer awareness of medical considerations
Payment records Trainers & Clients Subscription management via Razorpay
Trainer profile & tier Trainers Plan access and subscription level

I do not collect location data, contacts, or any device sensor data beyond what you explicitly provide.

03

How I Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve Trayn. Specifically:

— To provide trainers with the tools to manage their clients, assign workout and diet plans, and track progress.

— To give clients a personalised training experience including progress scoring, streak tracking, and coach feedback.

— To process payments securely through Razorpay.

— To send transactional emails (account-related notifications) via Resend.

— To improve the platform based on aggregated, anonymised usage patterns.

I do not sell your data. I do not use your data for advertising. I do not share your data with any third party except the infrastructure providers listed below.

04

Where Your Data Lives

All data is stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted in the ap-south-1 (Mumbai, India) AWS region. Your data does not leave India.

Progress photos and form check videos are stored in private Supabase storage buckets. They are not publicly accessible and are only visible to you and your assigned trainer.

Each of these providers has their own privacy policy. I encourage you to review them if you have concerns.

06

Data Retention

I retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all your personal data — including profile information, workout logs, diet logs, progress photos, and form check videos — is permanently deleted within 24 hours.

If you are a client and request data deletion without deleting your account, your trainer is notified and required to action the request within 30 days.

Payment records may be retained as required by Indian tax and financial regulations.

07

Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and applicable Indian law, you have the right to:

— Access the personal data I hold about you.

— Correct inaccurate or incomplete data via your profile settings.

— Delete your account and all associated data at any time from your profile screen.

— Request data deletion as a client without deleting your account, via the data deletion request flow in your profile.

— Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

— Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf.

To exercise any right or raise a concern, contact me at the email below. I will respond within 72 hours.

09

Children's Privacy

Trayn is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. I do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact me and I will delete the account promptly.

10

Security

I take security seriously. All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Database access is protected by Row Level Security (RLS) policies — meaning each user can only access their own data. Sensitive operations such as account deletion are handled server-side with restricted service role keys, not exposed to the client.

No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it to me responsibly at the email below.
