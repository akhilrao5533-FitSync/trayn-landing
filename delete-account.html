Account Management

Delete Your Account

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how.

Delete from the app

The fastest way to delete your account is directly from the Trayn app.

1

Open Trayn

Log in to your account at app.gettrayn.com or in the Trayn mobile app.

2

Go to Profile

Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.

3

Scroll to Account Settings

Scroll down to find the Account section at the bottom of your profile page.

4

Tap Delete Account

Tap "Delete Account" and confirm. For trainers, type "DELETE" to confirm. Your account and all data will be permanently removed within 24 hours.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed within 24 hours:

— Your profile information (name, email, phone, avatar)

— All workout logs and assigned plans

— All diet logs and assigned plans

— All progress photos and form check videos

— All session history and scheduling data

— All messages and chat history

— All client relationships (trainers) or trainer relationship (clients)

Payment records may be retained as required by Indian tax and financial regulations. All other personal data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

Request deletion by email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us directly. Include your registered email address in the request. We will process the deletion within 30 days.
